# Support Workflow Design

A case study of AI-assisted support workflows for researching incidents, preparing action plans, drafting customer updates, and checking whether fixes have been deployed.

The work separates investigation from communication. A planning stage organizes evidence, hypotheses, and unanswered questions into a brief; a drafting stage uses that brief to prepare an update for human review. A related release-checking design compares engineering status with deployment evidence.

**What this demonstrates:** breaking a complex support task into clear stages, carrying evidence between those stages, and keeping technical findings consistent with customer communication.

The case study presents the design using a fictional example. Organization-specific instructions and records are excluded; it documents the architecture rather than a standalone application.
